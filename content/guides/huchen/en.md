## Rules
- Only in the **winter season** (usually 1 October – 14 February) and only in sections that allow it.
- Often a **special permit**, a single hook, lures over 10 cm, a small annual quota or mandatory release.

## Gear
- Strong spinning rod 2.7–3.0 m, 30–80 g; 4000-size reel.
- Braid 0.18–0.25 mm + 0.50–0.60 mm fluorocarbon leader.
- A big landing net, or release the fish in the shallows – **never drag it onto dry land**.

## Lures
A 15–25 cm zopf on a jig head, big plugs, soft swimbaits, large streamers.

## When and where
- Cold days, **snowfall**, falling pressure, slightly coloured water.
- Deep pools and the edges of runs where the water slows; early morning and late afternoon.
- Persistence – huchen is "the fish of a thousand casts".

## Ethics
Huchen is an **endangered species**. Photograph quickly, support the fish in the water and let it go. Consider hiring an experienced guide.
