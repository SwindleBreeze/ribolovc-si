## Improved clinch
For hooks, spinners and swivels on mono.
1. Pass the line through the hook eye (10–15 cm).
2. Wrap the tag end 5–7 times around the main line.
3. Push the tag through the loop by the eye, then through the big new loop.
4. **Wet** it and pull tight slowly. Trim the tag.

## Palomar
The strongest simple knot – great for braid and drop-shot.
1. Double 15 cm of line and push the loop through the eye.
2. Tie a loose overhand knot with the doubled line.
3. Pass the loop over the hook or lure.
4. Wet, tighten, trim.

## Double uni
For joining braid to a fluorocarbon leader.
1. Overlap the two line ends by 20 cm.
2. With the first end tie a uni knot (4–6 turns) around the other line.
3. Repeat with the second end (8 turns for braid).
4. Wet, slide the knots together, trim.

> Tip: test every knot with a firm pull before you fish.
