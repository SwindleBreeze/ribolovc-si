## Gear
Rod 2.7–3.0 m, 10–40 g; 3000–4000 saltwater-proof reel, braid 0.10–0.14 + 0.30 mm fluorocarbon.

## Lures
- **Floating minnows** 9–14 cm – the core lure.
- **Topwater** (stickbait, popper) at dawn.
- **Soft baits** and **metal jigs** for long casts and deeper water.

## When
- **October–March**, with a **southerly wind (jugo)** and a slightly rough, coloured sea.
- **Dawn, dusk, night** and the turn of the tide.
- Check **wave height** and sea temperature in the 4-day forecast on the spot page.

## Where
Breakwaters, rocky points (Punta, Fiesa, Ankaran), the Rižana and Dragonja river mouths (outside protected areas).

## Safety
Wet rocks are slippery – wear good soles, and never fish a breakwater alone in big waves.
