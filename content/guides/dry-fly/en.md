## Gear
Rod 8.5–9 ft, #3–#5, floating line, 9 ft leader down to **0.12–0.14 mm**.

## When
- **Insect hatches** (mayflies, caddis) – late afternoon and evening from May to September.
- Watch for **rising fish** (rings on the water) – your cue for a dry fly.

## Technique
1. Pick a fly that matches the insects on the water (size matters more than colour).
2. Stand **below the fish** (downstream, slightly to the side) and cast 1 m above it.
3. The fly must drift **naturally, without drag** – mend your line.
4. When the fish takes – pause a moment (grayling!) then lift the rod gently.

## Favourite patterns
Elk Hair Caddis, Klinkhammer, Parachute Adams, CDC mayflies, a hopper for chub in summer.
