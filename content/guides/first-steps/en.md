## 1. Pick the right water
Start on **calm water with plenty of fish**: a lake or reservoir (Zbilje, Šmartno, Pernica, Velenje) or a put-and-take pond. Turn on the **Beginner friendly** filter on the map.

Alpine rivers (Soča, Sava Bohinjka, Unica) are stunning but demanding, and mostly fly-fishing only.

## 2. Buy a permit
In Slovenia you **always** need a permit for a specific fishing district (revir) to fish freshwater. Day permits can be bought **without a fishing exam** – online (ribiskekarte.si), from the local fishing club, tackle shops or tourist offices. See [Permits and the fishing exam](/en/guides/permits).

Shore fishing at sea needs no permit.

## 3. Basic gear (approx. €60–120)
- **Rod**: 2.7–3.6 m, medium action (10–40 g) – works for float fishing and spinning.
- **Reel**: size 2500–3000 with 0.20–0.25 mm mono.
- **Floats** 2–4 g, split shot, hooks size 10–16.
- **Bait**: maggots, worms, sweetcorn; a few size 2–3 spinners.
- **Landing net**, disgorger/forceps, scissors, a cloth.
- Polarised sunglasses – see into the water and protect your eyes.

## 4. Your first rig: the float
Start with [float fishing](/en/guides/float-rig). It's simple, cheap and catches almost everything from roach to carp.

## 5. When to go
Best **early morning or evening**. Check the **4-day forecast** on the spot page – the fishing score weighs air pressure, cloud, wind, rain, moon and river flow.

## 6. Etiquette
Take your litter home, keep your distance from other anglers and handle fish gently. Read [Etiquette and catch-and-release](/en/guides/etiquette).
