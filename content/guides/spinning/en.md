## Gear
- Rod 2.1–2.7 m; lure weight **2–10 g** (trout, perch), **10–40 g** (pike, zander).
- Reel 2000–3000, **braid** 0.08–0.14 mm + fluorocarbon leader.
- For pike a **wire or titanium trace is a must** (pike bite through mono).

## Choosing a lure
Use the **Which lure?** finder – pick species, water clarity and season.

| Situation | Lure |
|---|---|
| Clear water, sun | natural colours, smaller lures |
| Coloured water, overcast | gold, fluoro, vibration (spinner, shad) |
| Cold water | slow retrieve, near the bottom |
| Warm water | faster, higher in the water column |

## Technique
1. **Fan-cast** – cover every direction cast after cast.
2. Vary the **speed** and add short pauses – strikes often come on the pause.
3. On rivers cast **across or upstream** and work the lure with the current.
4. Keep hooks sharp and check your line often.

> On many trout sections spinning is banned or limited to a single barbless hook. Check the rules!
