## The rig
```
main line 0.18–0.22 mm
   │
 [float 1–4 g]  ← fixed with float rubbers or sliding with a stop knot
   │
 ●●● bulk shot (70 % of the load) ~40 cm above the hook
   │
   ● "tell-tale" shot ~10 cm above the hook
   │
  🪝 hook size 12–16 (carp 8–10)
```

## Steps
1. Thread the float, add the shot and tie on the hook ([clinch knot](/en/guides/knots)).
2. **Shot the float** so only the tip shows – you'll see even delicate bites.
3. **Plumb the depth**: clip a plummet to the hook, lower it and move the float until the tip just shows. Set the bait 5–10 cm off the bottom.
4. Cast and **feed a little** (maggots, corn) to the same spot.
5. When the float dips or slides away – **strike gently**.

## Tips
- In wind use a **waggler** (bottom-end-only float) and sink the line.
- For carp, stronger line (0.25 mm) and a size 8 hook with sweetcorn.
- At sea, catch mullet with bread under a float in harbours.
