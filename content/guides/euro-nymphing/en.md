## Gear
- Fly rod **10–11 ft**, #2–#4 weight.
- Thin line or a "mono rig" + **sighter** (coloured section).
- Tippet 0.12–0.16 mm, 1–2 **tungsten bead nymphs** size 12–18 (some sections allow only one fly – check).

## Technique
1. Get **close** (2–5 m) – casts are short.
2. Lob the nymph upstream, raise the rod and **lead the nymph at current speed** just off the bottom.
3. Watch the sighter – any hesitation, twitch or stop = **strike**.
4. Work each run systematically: near water first, then further out.

## Where
Runs 0.5–1.5 m deep, seams between fast and slow water, behind boulders. Grayling favour long even runs over gravel.

## Tips for Slovenia
- The Soča and Idrijca are gin-clear – use **finer tippet** and natural colours.
- After rain, when water is higher and coloured, try bigger, darker nymphs or a streamer.
