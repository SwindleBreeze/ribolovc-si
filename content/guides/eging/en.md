## Gear
- Light rod 2.4–2.7 m (up to 20 g), 2500 reel.
- **Braid 0.08–0.12** + 0.20–0.25 mm fluorocarbon (1–2 m).
- **Squid jigs (EGI)** size 2.0–3.0; some natural and some glow colours.

## Technique
<!-- diagram -->

1. Cast and let the jig **sink** (count it down to learn the depth).
2. Give **2–3 sharp upward jerks** of the rod.
3. Let the jig **glide down** on a tight line – squid attack on the drop.
4. When you feel weight or a "soft" stop – **reel steadily**, no jerks (tentacles can tear).

## When and where
- October–February, **dusk and night**, around **lit piers** (Izola, Piran, Koper, Portorož).
- Calm sea; natural colours under moonlight, glow in the dark.
- Cuttlefish in spring (March–May) – slowly near the bottom, also by day.

## Etiquette
Don't fish from moored boats or piers where it's forbidden; rinse the ink off the concrete.
