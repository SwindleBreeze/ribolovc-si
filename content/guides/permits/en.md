## Freshwater
All Slovenian freshwater is divided into **fishing districts** and **sections (revirji)** managed by **fishing clubs (ribiške družine)** or the Fisheries Research Institute. Each section has its own **rules**: permitted methods, baits, closed seasons, minimum sizes and daily bag limits.

### Day (tourist) permit
- Can be bought **without an exam**, foreigners included.
- Sold at **ribiskekarte.si** (online), fishing clubs, tackle shops, tourist offices and hotels.
- Valid for a specific section and day. The rules are printed on the permit – **read them**.
- **You must log your catch** (even a blank day) the way the section requires – on the permit or in the online system.

### Annual permit and membership
For an annual member permit you join a fishing club. This requires the **fishing exam (ribiški izpit)**, organised by the clubs (a course on fish biology, regulations and ethics). Youth and children often get discounts.

## Sea
- **From the shore**: recreational fishing **without a permit**.
- **From a boat**: you need the **annual sport sea-fishing permit** (issued by the state; see spot.gov.si).
- Respect **minimum sizes** (e.g. sea bass 25 cm, gilthead 23 cm) and no-fishing areas: **Strunjan**, **Sečovlje Salina**, **Debeli rtič**, ports and bathing areas.

## What if a warden stops me?
A fishing warden can check your permit, ID, catch and tackle. Carry everything with you. Violations are fined.

> Rules change. Before fishing, always check the section rules with the fishing club or on ribiskekarte.si.
