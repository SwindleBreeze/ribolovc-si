## Riverside etiquette
- **Take everything home** – other people's litter too, if you can. Discarded line kills birds.
- Keep your **distance** from other anglers (at least 20–30 m on a river); never step in ahead of someone already fishing.
- **Don't park** on meadows or farm tracks; respect private land.
- On rivers with rafters and kayakers, step back and wait.
- Don't light fires or camp where it isn't allowed.

## Catch-and-release done right
1. Use **barbless hooks** (or crush the barb with pliers).
2. Play the fish **quickly** – a long fight exhausts it.
3. Keep the fish **in the water**, or lift it only for a moment. Hands **wet**.
4. Don't squeeze the fish or touch its gills.
5. Remove the hook with forceps or a disgorger; cut the line if it is deeply hooked.
6. Hold the fish **facing into the current** until it swims off on its own.
7. When water is above **18 °C**, trout and grayling suffer – consider a break or another water.

## If you keep a fish
Respect the **minimum size** and **daily bag limit**. Dispatch the fish quickly and humanely and log the catch.
