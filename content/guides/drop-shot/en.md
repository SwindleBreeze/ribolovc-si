## The rig
```
braid 0.06–0.10 + fluorocarbon 0.20–0.25 mm
   │
 drop-shot hook size 2–6 (palomar knot, point up)
   │  ← 20–50 cm
 weight 5–15 g at the end
```
Nose-hook a **micro soft bait** of 5–8 cm (worms, minnows).

## Working it
1. Cast, let the weight settle, tighten the line.
2. **Shake the rod tip**, don't drag – the bait dances in place.
3. Every few seconds move the weight half a metre.
4. The bite is often just a "tick" – strike at once.

## Where
Jetties, rip-rap, concrete walls, deep water by bridges. Excellent in cold water when fish are sluggish.
