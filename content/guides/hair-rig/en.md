## Why a "hair"?
The bait (boilie) isn't on the hook but on a short hair below it. The carp sucks in the bait, the hook flips into its lip and **sets itself** against the lead.

## The rig
```
main line 0.30–0.35 mm
   │
 lead clip with 70–110 g lead
   │
 hooklink 15–25 cm (coated braid)
   │
 hook size 4–8 ─ hair 1–2 cm ─ boilie 15–20 mm
```

## Steps
1. Thread the boilie onto the hair with a baiting needle and lock it with a stop.
2. **Bait the swim** with pellets, corn and crushed boilies (or a PVA bag).
3. Put the rod on a pod with an **electronic bite alarm** and tighten the line.
4. When the alarm screams in one go (a "run") – lift the rod; the carp is already hooked.

## Must-haves
- An **unhooking mat** and a big landing net – never lay a carp on the ground.
- Night fishing only where the section allows it.
