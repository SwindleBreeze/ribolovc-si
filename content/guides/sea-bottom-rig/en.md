## The rig ("paternoster")
<!-- diagram -->

## Baits
Sea worms (the best all-rounder), mussels, shrimp, crabs (gilthead), sardine or squid chunks (conger).

## Steps
1. Cast onto sand near the edge of rocks or seagrass.
2. Tighten the line and watch the rod tip (or a bell).
3. Gilthead pick at the bait first – wait until the tip pulls round, then strike.

## When
Rising tide, dusk and night; gilthead around mussel farms and piers in autumn.

## Remember
Minimum sizes (gilthead 23 cm, white bream 23 cm, sand steenbras 20 cm, two-banded bream 18 cm, sole 20 cm) and no-fishing areas.
