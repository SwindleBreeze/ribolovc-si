## The rig
```
main line 0.22–0.28 mm (or 0.10 braid)
   │
 running/fixed feeder 20–60 g
   │
 swivel
   │
 hooklength 50–100 cm, 0.14–0.18 mm
   │
 hook size 10–16
```
Use a **feeder rod** (3.3–3.9 m) with soft quiver tips – the tip shows the bite.

## Steps
1. Fill the feeder with **groundbait** and maggots.
2. Cast to **exactly the same spot** every time (clip the line on the reel) to build a carpet of feed.
3. Cast often for the first 5–6 casts (every 2 min), then every 5–10 min.
4. When the tip pulls round rhythmically – lift into the fish.

## On rivers (barbel, chub)
A heavier feeder (60–120 g) to hold bottom; cheese, pellets or maggots for bait.

## For catfish
Heavy tackle (0.40+ mm line), a braided hooklength, 2/0–6/0 hook, a bunch of dew worms or pellets. At night near deep holes.
